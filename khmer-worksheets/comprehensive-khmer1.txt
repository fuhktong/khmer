2. Khmer: neang rean nau sala | English: She studies at school
3. Khmer: goad mean lan tmey | English: He has a new car
4. Khmer: bay nih kdaw nah | English: This rice is very hot
5. Khmer: knyom joal-jet aan seew-pow | English: I like to read books
6. Khmer: pteah nih tom hai-nung tmey | English: This house is big and new
7. Khmer: knyom dtow psa hai-nung neang nau pteah | English: I go to the market and she stays home
8. Khmer: goad rean nah bpon-dtae knyom layng | English: He studies a lot but I play
9. Khmer: knyom nyam bay bpee-bprooah klean nah | English: I eat rice because very hungry
10. Khmer: neang cheu bpon-dtae dtow twer ga-ngea | English: She is sick but goes to work
11. Khmer: knyom jong gayng bpon-dtae dtrow rean | English: I want to sleep but must study
12. Khmer: goad pak dtuk bpee-bprooah kdaw nah | English: He drinks water because very hot
13. Khmer: knyom dtow sala hai-nung joub met-peak | English: I go to school and meet friends
14. Khmer: neang mean seew-pow tmey hai-nung aan | English: She has a new book and reads
15. Khmer: knyom rean nau sala bpayl-pruk hai-nung twer ga-ngea bpayl-l'ngeaj | English: I study at school in the morning and work in the evening
16. Khmer: goad nyam dtrey kdaw bpon-dtae neang joal-jet nyam saj-moan | English: He eats hot fish but she likes to eat chicken
17. Khmer: knyom dtow psa sa-aik bpee-bprooah jong dtinh plae-cher tmey | English: I go to the market tomorrow because want to buy new fruit
18. Khmer: neang mean pteah tom hai-nung lan tmey nah la-or | English: She has a big house and a very good new car
19. Khmer: knyom pak cafe kdaw bpayl-pruk hai-nung pak bi-yea bpayl-l'ngeaj | English: I drink hot coffee in the morning and drink beer in the evening
20. Khmer: knyom rean hai-nung twer bpon-dtae goad nau pteah gayng | English: I study and work but he stays home sleeping
